## Practical model

Measured fall velocities are taken from the worked example (v = 27.52, 37.77, 53.0 cm/s for r = 0.1, 0.225, 0.325 cm),
interpolated linearly in r² for other radii. The sphere settles to that velocity with a 0.03 s time constant
and slows within 3 cm of the base. With D = 35.5 cm the timed values come out 1.29, 0.94 and 0.67 s.
